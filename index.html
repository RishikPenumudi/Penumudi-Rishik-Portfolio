03 / FEATURED CASE STUDY

SAHAJ: tradition, with control.

A project at the intersection of heritage, sustainability, and engineering — developed and presented with Team Zyric.

01Process variablesTemperature · time · stirring · pH
02Prototype componentsArduino · sensor · relays · pump system
03Design goalMore repeatable, eco-conscious dyeing
THE CHALLENGE

Natural dyeing can be hard to standardize.

Colour outcomes may vary with process conditions such as temperature, timing, pH, and stirring. SAHAJ explores ways to make these variables easier to manage while supporting natural dyeing.

THE APPROACH

Prototype around key process variables.

The prototype explored Arduino-based control, a temperature sensor, relays, pumps, a motor, and a heating-load simulation. The compact 3D-printed model includes a processing vessel and a control interface.

01SenseTemperature
→
02ControlTime · Stirring · pH
→
03ObserveProcess consistency
Recognition: Smart India Hackathon 2025 National Finalist · upgraded prototype presented at K L University SIH 2026 internal round · SAHAJ presented at PitchX 2026 in Mumbai.
Future directions discussed include IoT batch logging, mobile control, AI-assisted colour recommendations, automated dye quantity detection, and solar-powered adaptation. These are proposed enhancements, not claims of completed features.
