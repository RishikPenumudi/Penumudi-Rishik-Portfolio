FLAGSHIP INNOVATION PROJECT · TEAM ZYRIC

SAHAJ — Smart Automated Heritage Artisan Natural Dyeing System

An automated hardware-software prototype blending embedded process automation, thermal control, and an AI-assisted roadmap to empower natural textile artisans.

Primary Prototype Photograph (SIH'25 Finalist Iteration) Stainless-steel extraction vessel, touchscreen control interface, cream enclosure, silicone transfer tubing, and electronics bay.

TECHNICAL REFERENCE

Hardware Layout & Electronics

Physical Enclosure & Subsystem Detail Internal layout showcasing solid-state relay modules, thermal regulation circuitry, and peristaltic pumping lines.

The Critical Problem

Synthetic chemical dyeing produces massive volumes of toxic effluent, consuming extensive energy and heavy metals. Meanwhile, traditional botanical dyeing faces challenges in temperature repeatability, measurement accuracy, process-time tracking, and color variance across artisan batches.

The System Vision

SAHAJ introduces precision-regulated botanical extraction: controlled heating profiles, digital temperature feedback, automated stirring, and timed cloth immersion. Artisans achieve consistent, repeatable shades without losing the heritage essence of natural dyes.

Sustainable Business Model

To maximize artisan adoption, the business model avoids heavy hardware capital expenses. The system is designed around supplying standardized, pre-measured natural dye inputs and consumable cartridges, keeping the core machinery affordable for rural clusters.

AI Integration Vision · 4 Dedicated Goals

Proposed algorithmic roadmap to assist artisans through intelligent estimation and closed-loop process guidance.

GOAL 01
Colour Palette Identification

Analyzes desired color shades or fabric targets, mapping them to validated botanical dye formulations (e.g. madder root, marigold, pomegranate rind).

GOAL 02
Biomass & Water Measurement

Calculates precise solvent-to-plant biomass ratios based on batch volume, fabric dry-weight, and water hardness for optimal extraction efficiency.

GOAL 03
Dye Output Prediction

Estimates chromatic yield and concentration prior to immersion, computing expected error margins and recommending real-time adjustments.

GOAL 04
Process Guidance & Immersion

Monitors extraction kinetics over time, alerting the artisan when extraction has reached equilibrium and initiating the fabric immersion cycle.

Development Status & Integrity Note: The physical prototype shown above successfully validates temperature regulation, stirring, vessel pumping, and basic color feedback. The four AI workflow goals represent our active algorithmic roadmap and are clearly distinguished from current hardware-validated features.
